About

Standing for Humanity in Owerri, Imo State

Stand For Humanity Foundation is a humanitarian organization based in Owerri, Imo State, Nigeria, committed to protecting vulnerable people, defending those whose voices are often unheard, and providing support to individuals and families facing difficult circumstances.

Our Mission

To protect the vulnerable, give voice to the voiceless, rescue those in danger, support victims of abuse and violence, and provide meaningful assistance to people in need.
